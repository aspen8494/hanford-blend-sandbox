About this sandbox

A scaled-down, fully client-side cousin of the campaign builder: pack waste blends into a volume band under WAC constraints and watch the glass mass respond. All computation runs in your browser — no server, no data leaves this page.

Data: 38 App-D DFHLW feed layers (+ retrieval heels) with per-layer oxide vectors and WAP values from RPP-RPT-64878-01; glass property models from PNNL-35884 Rev. 1 (EWG2.5). The sandbox reproduces the full application's campaign counts, waste totals, glass mass, and canister counts within a few percent (see the repo's parity tests). A marketing overview, not an engineering basis: results here are computed with the same algorithms but a reduced data scope.
